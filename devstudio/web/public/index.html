    Ambivo review

    Ambivo reviews every app before a client can install it. A program and an assistant check the code, the services it calls and what it shows. If they find a problem, you get the list by email. If everything passes, Ambivo staff decide. We email you at each step.

    You can withdraw a version until staff decide.

    Apps about gambling, sexual content, or content unsuitable for children are refused. Apps for children are welcome when everything in them suits children.

    Each version is reviewed once. After any change, raise the version in src/install/app-objects.json and submit again.

    Deploy to a client

    Make a link and send it to your client's Ambivo admin. They sign in with their own account, see the custom objects the app adds, and click Install. You never see their password. Their company must be on the Pro plan.

    Jobs

    Jobs run on a schedule in Ambivo, even when nobody has the app open. They are saved in src/install/app-jobs.json and installed with the app.
